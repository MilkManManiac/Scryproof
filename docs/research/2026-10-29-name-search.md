## What it means for us

- The company and product name Scryproof is clear as far as a non-lawyer search goes. Scry stays the nickname.
- Calling the product "Scry" on its own would sit next to a live Class 42 SaaS registration for the identical word, plus two active unregistered software companies. An examiner compares marks as a whole by sound, look and meaning, so a Scryproof application could still have SCRY cited against it; a distinct compound word has the better odds.
- USPTO base filing fee is $350 per class (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/how-much-does-it-cost). Common-law rights come from use; state LLC registration is not a trademark; federal registration covers the whole US (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-register-your-trademark).
- A trademark attorney would settle the Class 42 question in an hour. That is the three members' money decision, made by them.
